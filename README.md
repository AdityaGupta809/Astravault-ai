# 🚀 AstraVault AI  
### Trust-Aware Offline Knowledge Retrieval System

AstraVault AI is a secure, offline-first Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) knowledge portal designed for environments where privacy, reliability, and trustworthiness matter more than raw completeness. The system enables natural-language querying over internal organizational documents while enforcing **role-based access control** and **trust-conditioned response generation** — ensuring only **citation-backed, high-confidence answers** are delivered.

> ⚖️ Design Principle: **Accuracy over Completeness**

---

## 🔍 Problem Statement

Organizations working in security-sensitive domains (e.g., R&D labs, aerospace, defense, policy, healthcare) require AI-powered knowledge tools — but cloud-based LLMs pose major risks:

- ❌ Sensitive data cannot leave internal networks  
- ❌ Unknown trustworthiness of AI responses  
- ❌ No guarantee of compliance-safe outputs  
- ❌ Traditional keyword search lacks reasoning ability  

**AstraVault AI solves this — completely offline.**

4️⃣ Trust confidence checks are applied
